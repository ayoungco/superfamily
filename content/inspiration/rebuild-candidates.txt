Old LEGO Shop@Home order note (merged from a separate scrap file, ~2001, via BZPower)

You read right - the sets originally scheduled for August are available for order now in the US (and possibly elsewhere) through calling LEGO Shop@Home. They're not on the web site yet, so you have to call.

Dial 1-800-835-4386 and hit zero to talk with someone to order. The following sets are available:

Item 8593 Makuta (US$19.99)
Item 8594 Gukko Bird with Jala (US$19.99)
Item 8595 Pewku Crab with Takua (US$19.99)
Item 8596 Takanuva with Ussanui (US$29.99)

These items won't show up on the S@H web site for another week, so the only way you can get them is by calling and asking by name or part number.
