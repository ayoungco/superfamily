# Dr. Seinyor

Related: [Canon IDs](../../docs/canon-ids.md) | [[The exact details of the Super Family]] | [[Dr. Seinyor]] | [[The Return of Dr. Seinyor]]

Founder and father of the Super Family, registered Raven AC pilot, and wielder of the
Unlimiter. A human in a world where humans and LEGO people live together. He started as a
mocked, lonely explorer and became the hero who united heroes from across the multiverse.

## Origin

- **The gold disk.** On a solo expedition he found a gold plate cut with pulsing runes in a
  small monument under a waterfall (see [[Iguazu Falls]]). His museum presentation of it
  in Legoredo was a disaster, and he set out to sea on his rickety ship as a hermit.
- **The rescue.** During the great flood he took Stacie (`lore:stacie`) and her brother
  Pepper off a mountaintop. He wrecked the ship on Lego Island (`lore:lego-island`) and
  settled there, building the House beside Mountain Park.
- **"You... are... chosen..."** Touching a white rune on the disk threw him into a lightning
  waterspout. He woke with flight, enormous strength and an enhanced intellect, then
  planned the capture of the Brickster. That victory made the trio famous.
- He trained Stacie and adopted her. After that he could teach powers directly, so the disk
  was no longer needed to make heroes.

## Eras

1. **The Powerteam** (`lore:era-powerteam`). The original team: Dr. Seinyor, Stacie, Pepper,
   the Powerpuff Girls (whom he adopted), and later Padmé and Anakin. He learned the Force
   and the lightsaber from Qui-Gon Jinn, became Pokémon champion in Kanto, and banished the
   Evil Brother into the Portal of Chaos. He made Butch, Boomer and Brick as sons. Also
   hosted the Powerteam show on tape (`lore:powerteam-show`).
2. **The Super Army** (`lore:era-super-army`). He sent invitations in three waves (Crash,
   Gex and Spyro; the heroes of Mossflower; new allies like Louis the Trumpeter Swan),
   built a droid army modelled on the Trade Federation's, and built the AC battle station
   (`lore:ac-battle-station`). He married Nell Aulter (`lore:nell-aulter`) at the
   station's christening. Boar the badger named the Super Army at the reception.
3. **The Super Family** (`lore:era-super-family`). His Portal Room test accidentally let
   Makuta (`lore:makuta`) out of Mata Nui (`lore:mata-nui`), which starts the movies. Aku
   Aku called them one big family, and the name stuck.

## Powers and skills

- Flight (he can switch gravity off), super strength, and an intellect boosted by the disk.
- The Force and lightsaber combat, learned from Qui-Gon Jinn.
- Pokémon Master; his team appears in *Dr. Seinyor's Pokémon* (below).
- The best AC pilot of the team, with many part configurations; a registered Raven.
- In combat ([SFCOMBAT](../lore/SFCOMBAT.txt)) he is the finisher. The team wears the
  enemy down, and the Pulse of Vengeance rises as members fall. Hyper Supernova Beam can
  only be charged when he is the last fighter standing. Only he and Takanuva have filled
  the Pulse bar.
- After his return: a spirit form that can possess and strengthen people with heroic
  intent, which takes a long time to recharge.

## Equipment

- **The Unlimiter** (`lore:unlimiter`). The gold disk. It powered the Ship and then the AC,
  opened portals anywhere in space and time, and after every member signed it, it was
  reforged and named the Unlimiter.
- **The Ship** (`lore:the-ship`) and the **AC battle station** (`lore:ac-battle-station`):
  the House in the head, the army in the legs.
- **The Interversal Atlas** (`lore:interversal-atlas`): an experimental device and his only
  guide home after the fall.
- **Dr. Seinyor's Suitcase**: drawn twice, with its contents (art below).

## Relationships

- **Family:** adoptive father of Stacie, Blossom, Bubbles and Buttercup; maker and father of
  Butch, Boomer and Brick; husband of Nell Aulter.
- **Rivals:** the Evil Brother (`lore:evil-brother`), his twin, and the younger Evil Sister.
  Makuta, the Super Family's main enemy in the movies.
- **Allies:** Boar the badger (named the Super Army), Sunflash (placed the reforged plate,
  researched the Drive gauge), Aku Aku, the Toa.

## The fall and return

The chronicle *Mutunga* in [[The Return of Dr. Seinyor]] (`lore:mutunga`) tells it. Overuse
of the Unlimiter let the Evil Brother track the AC. His operatives disabled the Unlimiter
and struck Dr. Seinyor; no body was found. The Super Family disbanded, the AC was parked in
orbit around the LEGO planet, and the Heroes Legacy Preservation Society still looks for
him. A spatial "crack" from that moment orbits the star.

The shattered Unlimiter's shards drove him through the Interverse into the Warcraft
universe, into the body of a Forsaken warlock named Irascorus. The fall of the Lich King
freed his spirit. His plan: go on to The Elder Scrolls and consult an Elder Scroll to find
the way home.
