# Original Character Art for the Human Cast (idea)

Related: [[Dr. Seinyor]] | [[The exact details of the Super Family]] | [Site Archive Wiki Revival](site-archive-wiki-revival.md) | [Combat System as a Playable Game](combat-system-game.md)

## The idea

`content/site-archive/images/` leans on borrowed franchise art (Bionicle
character renders like `bahrag.png`, `brutaka.png`, `makuta.png`) as
stand-ins, including for portraits like `evilbrother.png` that represent
original, non-licensed characters. The Super Family's actual originals —
Dr. Seinyor, Stacie, Pepper, Aleena, the Evil Brother, the Evil Sister,
Nell Aulter — don't have art of their own, only borrowed likenesses used
because that's what was available at the time.

## Sketch

- Generate consistent original portraits for the human OCs, grounded in
  the descriptions already written in [[Dr. Seinyor]] and
  [[The exact details of the Super Family]].
- Consistency across characters and across multiple generations (so
  Dr. Seinyor looks like the same person in a portrait, a battle pose,
  and a title card) is the hard part — needs a reference sheet or
  similar per-character conditioning approach, not one-off generations.
- These would directly feed [Site Archive Wiki Revival](site-archive-wiki-revival.md)
  (character pages) and [Combat System as a Playable Game](combat-system-game.md)
  (fighter portraits for anyone without existing art).

## Open questions

- Art style — matching the late-90s/2000s LEGO-and-anime-crossover
  sensibility of the original influences vs. a more modern illustration
  style.
- Whether borrowed franchise images stay in place for licensed
  characters/cameos (Crash Bandicoot, the Powerpuff Girls, etc.) and
  only the true originals get new art, or everything gets a consistent
  new treatment.
