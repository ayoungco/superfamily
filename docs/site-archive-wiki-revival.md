# Site Archive Wiki Revival (idea)

Related: [Crossover Universe Knowledge Graph](crossover-knowledge-graph.md) | [Original Character Art](original-character-art.md)

## The idea

`content/site-archive/` is a hand-built fan wiki from the original era
of the project, not just scraps — `index.html`, `history.html`,
`battlesystem.html`, `evilies.html`, `items_with_WoW_rarities.html`,
plus a full `images/` set (character portraits, gauge art, an
`ac.gif`/`intro.gif`/`evil.gif` set) and its own `sf.css`. It's sitting
as static, non-responsive, effectively unopened HTML.

## Sketch

- Rebuild it responsively, keeping the original visual identity
  (`sf.css`, the icon/portrait set) rather than starting over with a
  generic template — the point is nostalgia, not a redesign.
- Backfill it with everything since extracted: per-episode metadata and
  synopses from `content/data/episode-metadata/`, character pages
  grounded in the transcripts instead of just the original hand-written
  blurbs, and a page for the item-rarity system already sketched in
  `items_with_WoW_rarities.html`.
- If [Crossover Universe Knowledge Graph](crossover-knowledge-graph.md)
  gets built, it's a natural page inside this wiki rather than a
  standalone artifact.

## Open questions

- Static site generator vs. a single hand-rolled artifact — depends on
  how much content ends up here.
- Hosted somewhere durable vs. kept local-only; this is personal
  archival material, not something to publish without deciding that
  deliberately.
- How much of the original hand-written content (`history.html`,
  `evilies.html`) to preserve verbatim vs. fold into the newer,
  transcript-grounded material — verbatim preservation probably wins,
  with new material added alongside rather than replacing it.
