### The Recommended Workflow

Instead of typing everything manually, use **Rebrickable**. It is the gold standard for this exact need.

1. **Register:** Create an account on [Rebrickable](https://rebrickable.com/).
    
2. **Import:** You can bulk-add your collection by set number.
    
3. **Export:** Once your sets are in, the platform allows you to export your entire inventory as a CSV/Excel file. This gives you the parts list, color counts, and set distributions automatically.
